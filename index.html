How pure water cleaning works

Cleaner windows that stay clean for longer.

No soap means no sticky film left behind for dirt to cling to.

  1. 01

    Filter

    Tap water is filtered until every mineral is gone. Pure water dries clear, without spots or streaks.

  2. 02

    Scrub

    We scrub the glass and frames from the ground with a soft brush on a water-fed pole.

  3. 03

    Rinse

    A final rinse carries the dirt away. The windows dry naturally to a clear finish.
